    Documentation Intelligence

    PRIVATE PREVIEW # AI-Powered Documentation Intelligence ### Documentation is evolving. Your tools should too. An upcoming enterprise platform that helps documentation teams understand product changes, uncover content gaps, improve documentation quality, and make smarter documentation decisions through AI.
    [Request Early Access](request-access.md){ .md-button .md-button--primary } [Contact Me](../../contact/){ .md-button }

    Why Documentation Intelligence?

    Documentation has become one of the most critical assets for modern software companies.

    Yet many teams still rely on manual reviews, disconnected tools, spreadsheets, and individual expertise to maintain documentation quality.

    As products grow, understanding what changed, what documentation is affected, and what needs attention becomes increasingly difficult.

    This platform is being built to help documentation teams make informed decisions through AI-powered intelligence instead of manual effort.

    How It Helps

    - :material-file-document-outline: ### Understand Documentation Impact Quickly identify documentation that may be affected by product changes, helping teams focus their review efforts where it matters most. - :material-book-search-outline: ### Discover Knowledge Gaps Gain visibility into missing or incomplete documentation to improve product adoption and customer self-service. - :material-text-box-search-outline: ### Improve Content Quality Surface opportunities to strengthen documentation quality, consistency, and usability across your knowledge base. - :material-graph-outline: ### Measure Documentation Health Understand the overall health of your documentation through meaningful insights instead of manual audits. - :material-account-group-outline: ### Enable Better Collaboration Support documentation, product, engineering, and support teams with a shared understanding of documentation readiness. - :material-lightbulb-on-outline: ### Make Better Documentation Decisions Turn documentation into a measurable business asset rather than a collection of static articles.

    Documentation deserves more than search.

    Modern documentation is more than a collection of help articles.

    As products evolve, documentation teams face increasing challenges in keeping content accurate, consistent, discoverable, and aligned with every product change.

    Our vision is to help documentation teams transform documentation into an intelligent, measurable, and continuously improving knowledge ecosystem.

    Designed for

    • Technical Writers
    • Documentation Managers
    • Product Teams
    • Support Organizations
    • Customer Success
    • Knowledge Managers

    Platform Vision

    Rather than generating documentation, the platform focuses on helping teams understand the health, quality, impact, and evolution of their documentation throughout the product lifecycle.

    Built For

    - **Technical Writers** Create and maintain high-quality documentation more efficiently. - **Documentation Managers** Gain visibility into documentation quality, impact, and team productivity. - **Product Teams** Understand how product changes influence customer-facing documentation. - **Support Teams** Improve knowledge quality and reduce repetitive customer questions. - **Customer Success** Help customers find accurate, up-to-date documentation. - **Engineering Teams** Collaborate with documentation teams throughout the product lifecycle.

    Why This Platform?

    Most documentation tools help teams create documentation.

    This platform is designed to help teams understand documentation.

    It focuses on answering questions such as:

    - **What documentation is impacted by a product change?** - **Where are the knowledge gaps?** - **How healthy is our documentation?** - **What should be reviewed before a release?** - **Which documentation should be prioritized?** - **How can documentation teams make better decisions using AI?**

    Instead of replacing technical writers, the platform is being built to help documentation teams work more efficiently, make informed decisions, and maintain high-quality documentation at scale.
